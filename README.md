### Consulting & Analytics Club — IIT Guwahati

## Overview

This project applies time series forecasting techniques to build a 
data-driven virtual stock portfolio on the StockGro platform. Using 
five years of historical NSE stock data (January 2021 – December 2025), 
three forecasting models were built and compared — ARIMA, Facebook Prophet, 
and LSTM — and their predictions were used to allocate a virtual capital 
of ₹10,00,000 across seven NSE-listed stocks.

The complete workflow covers data fetching, preprocessing, modeling, 
portfolio construction, live virtual trading, and post-trade performance 
analysis — all documented in a single end-to-end Jupyter notebook.

---

## Problem Statement

**Data-Driven Stock Analysis using Time Series Models on StockGro**

Given five years of daily closing price data for a selected universe of NSE 
stocks, can time series forecasting models generate trading signals that 
translate into positive portfolio returns over a two-day live trading window?

## Models Used

### 1. ARIMA
- Auto-selected best order (p, d, q) using AIC grid search
- Applied first differencing after ADF stationarity tests
- Best orders ranged from (0,1,0) to (3,1,3) depending on the stock

### 2. Facebook Prophet
- Weekly and yearly seasonality enabled
- Captures long-term structural trends and calendar effects
- Used `next_2_days` forecast for trading signals

### 3. LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)
- 60-day lookback window on Min-Max scaled data
- Best performance on MAPE and RMSE among all three models
- Captures non-linear dynamics in price series

### Ensemble
Final trading signals were generated by averaging predictions 
from all three models to reduce individual model bias.

## Portfolio Construction

Two strategies were combined with equal 50-50 weighting:

- **Strategy A (Forecast-Guided):** Ranked stocks by predicted 
  2-day ensemble return and allocated proportionally to 
  positive-return stocks
- **Strategy B (Volatility-Aware):** Assigned weights inversely 
  proportional to each stock's 30-day rolling volatility 
  (lower volatility = higher allocation)

## Key Findings

- LSTM consistently outperformed ARIMA and Prophet on price 
  level accuracy (MAPE) across all stocks
- The ensemble approach improved directional accuracy to 64.3% 
  over the two-day window combined
- Sector diversification kept the total 2-day drawdown to just 
  -0.39% despite mixed individual stock performance
- The primary limitation was the 5-month gap between the model 
  training period (Dec 2025) and the trading window (May 2026), 
  which caused high absolute price errors despite reasonable 
  directional predictions
